# VAT breakdown from the shop's own prices
Objective: make the VAT the TPV splits, prints and records the VAT PrestaShop actually applies, instead of the hardcoded 21% constant, without changing the amount charged. Branch: `fix/ttc-price-basis` (continues the TTC basis feature). No PrestaShop module change; no deployment.

## Specs
S1. "no se puede cambiar nada en prestashop solo en este proyecto" — any fix must live in this repository and must not require deploying the `tpvconnect` module.
S2. "el prestahop que esta funcionando es como biene por defecto, es probable que ni tenga las reglas de iva bien" — the VAT the TPV reports must come from the shop's own prices, not from an assumption, so a different rate (10%, 4%, 0%) or mixed rates stay correct.
S3. "haz lo que consideres mas optimo" — decide and implement; delivery (push, pull request, merge) remains the user's decision.
S4. Verified behaviour: `/api/products/{id}?price[a][use_tax]=1&price[a][use_reduction]=1&price[b][use_tax]=0&price[b][use_reduction]=1` returns both the tax-included and the tax-excluded reduced price in ONE request, for a product and for a combination with `product_attribute` (product 1: ttc 23.14 / net 19.12; product 3: ttc 35.09 / net 29.00; combination 1 of product 1: ttc 23.14 / net 19.12).
S5. The amount charged must never change because of this task: the VAT split is reporting only.
